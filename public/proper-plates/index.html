About Proper Plates

“Butter, but Better.” Chef Markus began by crafting flavored butters — a simple idea that blossomed into a full-service culinary project. Fueled by a love of food and hospitality, we create thoughtful bites and memorable experiences, sharing a taste of Napa Valley through a seasoned aspiring chef’s lens.

By Markus Daimeion

The Real Deal

From wine-country tastings to neighborhood pop-ups, we’re always refining our craft and dialing in flavors. Ask about our seasonal menus and custom tastings.

Pop-Ups

We celebrate the flavors of the world — one plate at a time. Whether your location has a kitchen or not, we show up ready to cook. Pop-Ups are our specialty, and we’d love to join you for your next event.

Private Chef

From intimate wine dinners to multi-course tasting menus, Chef Markus brings restaurant-quality polish to your home or vacation rental. We’ll design a seasonal menu around your preferences and pair it with relaxed, attentive service.
