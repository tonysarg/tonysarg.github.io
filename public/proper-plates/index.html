About Proper Plates

What started with Chef Markus whipping up small-batch flavored butters has grown into pop-ups, retail goods, and full-service events all over Napa Valley. We show up ready to cook — your place, our flavors. From farmers markets to intimate private dinners and corporate events, everything we make is thoughtful, bold, and rooted in genuine hospitality. You want great food and a memorable experience? Say less. We got you.

By Markus Daimeion

The Real Deal

From wine-country tastings to neighborhood pop-ups, we’re always refining our craft and dialing in flavors. Ask about our seasonal menus and custom tastings.

Pop-Ups

We celebrate the flavors of the world — one plate at a time. Whether your location has a kitchen or not, we show up ready to cook. Pop-Ups are our specialty, and we’d love to join you for your next event.

Private Chef

From intimate wine dinners to multi-course tasting menus, Chef Markus brings restaurant-quality polish to your home or vacation rental. We’ll design a seasonal menu around your preferences and pair it with relaxed, attentive service.

Perfect for: anniversaries, small celebrations, winemaker dinners, and chef’s-table experiences.

Taste Napa at Home

Tell us your vibe — rustic Italian, market-fresh Californian, or playful small-plates — and we’ll build the evening around it.

Bites, Boards & Stations

Wine-bar snacks, passed bites, sandwich stations, salad spreads — we’ll tailor the format to your space and guest count.

Catering

Full-service food handling & preparation for events of many kinds — from sampling stations to outdoor summer parties. Need bites for a wine event? Sandwiches for a community tasting? Say less.

Ask about rentals, staffing, serviceware, and dietary accommodations.

Chef’s Pantry

Prepared ingredients and ready-to-enjoy foods, made in small batches: compound (flavored) butters, pickled vegetables, grab-and-go salads, meal kits, and more. Inquire for the current list with handling/serving notes and delivery options.

Meal Kits
